WRAITH with GN Games catalog and SVG launcher.
Upload index.html, style.css, app.js, games.js, demo.html and WRAITH.svg into the ROOT of your PUBLIC radrich123110-ai/wraith repository. Replace the old files, commit changes, and wait for deployment.
Then open https://radrich123110-ai.github.io/wraith/ or https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/radrich123110-ai/wraith@main/WRAITH.svg
The SVG fetches your own repository index.html and loads it with the WRAITH interface. GN games are fetched as remote HTML links; some may block embedding or need other assets. The catalog filters certain age-inappropriate titles.
